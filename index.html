</> About FIXnBITS

Small workshop. Big capability. Serving Grimsby, Cleethorpes & Lincolnshire.

FIXnBITS is an independent repair workshop based in Grimsby & Cleethorpes, specialising in computers, laptops, games consoles and electronics repair at component level for customers across Lincolnshire. Where other shops say "it's dead, buy a new one", we get the multimeter, the hot air station and the microscope out.

We handle everything from everyday jobs — screen swaps, DC jack replacements, virus removal and full internal console cleans — through to advanced micro-soldering: HDMI port replacements, broken traces, lifted pads and surface-mount component work.

Every repair starts with a proper diagnosis, a clear quote and no surprises. Most repairs carry our no fix, no fee promise, and we use quality parts backed by a workmanship warranty.

Whether you're in Grimsby, Cleethorpes or further afield in Lincolnshire — from Immingham and Healing to Louth and beyond — drop us a repair request and we'll get your tech sorted.
